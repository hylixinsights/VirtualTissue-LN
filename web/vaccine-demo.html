What drives this demonstration?

Each recorded round uses the existing LN kernel: local antigen access, receptor matching, costimulation, physical contact, processing clocks and finite help. Two finite antigen packets challenge an already neighboring cognate DC–CD4–B trio. All nonparticipating cells choose WAIT. The scripted B-cell policy favors the eligible plasmablast route.

The DC primes CD4; the B cell captures native antigen and presents its own peptide to the helper. The same B cell then differentiates and secretes IgM. Antibody icons illustrate recorded secretion units, not individual molecule counts. This is an extrafollicular output demonstration, not germinal-center maturation or a prediction of vaccine efficacy.
